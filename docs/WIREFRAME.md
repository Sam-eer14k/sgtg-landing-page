# SG Technology Group: section plan

**Visitor:** an owner or office manager at a small business in New York,
Connecticut or New Jersey. Something is broken, slow or insecure, and they want
one dependable IT partner instead of a different vendor for each problem.

**Page's job:** get them to request a callback or call 347.674.8324.

**Main worry to answer:** "Will this be another IT company that bills me and
leaves the problem half fixed?" The answers SGTG can stand behind: the 100%
money-back guarantee on repairs, and personal service from Solomon Greene, in
IT since 1993.

**Proof rules:** only facts published on sgtechgroup.com. Anything else is
marked `[CONFIRM: ...]` (see the README for the full list).

**Calls to action:** one primary style (blue, angled corners) that leads to the
form or the symptom picker; one secondary style (outline) that is always
click-to-call.

## What each section does

| Section | Visitor question | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | "Is this for a business like mine?" | Name the pain, give two ways to act, show the guarantee and 1993 straight away. |
| How we work with you | "What does working with them look like?" | Three stages, each linking into the services it covers. |
| About | "Who's behind this?" | A real name, a real track record, and a diagram that shows where SGTG fits in an office. |
| Stats | "How established are they?" | Four checkable facts, no made-up counts. |
| Services | "Can they handle my problem?" | All seven services in SGTG's own words, each with a CTA that fills in the form. |
| Why businesses stay | "Why them and not someone else?" | The four reasons clients come back. |
| Symptom picker | "Where do I even start?" | Turn a vague problem into the right service and a pre-filled request. |
| What we support / Industries | "Will they work on our setup?" | Concrete systems and the kinds of offices SGTG serves. |
| Trusted by | "Who else uses them?" | Client names from the Clients page. |
| Calculator / Checklist | "Is this worth fixing now?" | Let the visitor put a number on downtime and spot security gaps. |
| Comparison | "Why not just call a repair guy?" | Show what planned support adds. |
| Process | "What happens after I call?" | Removes uncertainty about the first step. |
| Case results | "Has it worked for others?" | Before/after stories, once the client supplies them. |
| Reviews | "Do others trust them?" | Four named testimonials, verbatim. |
| FAQ | "Guarantee? Who shows up? My area?" | Answers the last objections, with a call-us fallback. |
| Contact | "How do I reach them?" | Short, validated form plus the phone number. |
